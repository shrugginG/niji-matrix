# TA0007 final-inventory convergence review

This is a bounded research review against Enterprise19.1 and the current canonical matrix. Admission and acceptance counts are finalized in integration-summary.yaml and final-verification.json; this document does not pre-empt pending candidate gates or human approval.

## Direct behavior and information boundaries

The final five close specific remaining inventory questions: who owns or uses a system, which services and scheduled registrations exist, what time facts a source reports, which VMs are managed under host/hypervisor authority, and whether local evidence causes an analysis-conditioned successor. These are not interchangeable with account sets, running processes, installed products, drivers, cloud resource graphs, location inference, credential acquisition or later execution.

Current-identity names, token SIDs, real/effective UIDs, sessions and artifact ownership require provenance. Service/task registration is not execution, and pure driver-directed queries retain NJT-0180. Date-time, timezone, uptime, monotonic counters and hardware counts require their own domains and units; display timezone is not device location. Registered VMIDs, running World IDs and managed-object references have different authorities and lifetimes. Acquired facts do not establish subsequent service stopping, encryption, core-payload execution or an actor's identity.

NJT-0122 is the existing owner of local analysis-environment conditioned evasion. Its Discovery admission must reuse that identity and its reviewed native taxonomy while preserving Stealth membership and the original decision. Plain discovery is an input, not automatically evidence of a gate. Debugger observations, pure delay and positive target matching retain NJT-0106, NJT-0107 and NJT-0109. Repairing its experiment contracts and evidence modes is required before cross-tactic reuse; it cannot be replaced by a cloned Discovery parent.

## Open search for an additional native Discovery parent

The review examined information object, acquisition source, observation position, task carriage, existing results, query selection, event timing, representation and successor conditions. It found no presently supported additional parent with a direct behavior not already owned by the fixed-parent outcomes and adjacent canonical techniques. This is a conclusion limited to the examined evidence, not a claim that every possible stealth implementation has been discovered.

- Concrete inline task carriage has independent identity- and service-query evidence and distinct native defense contracts. A generic resident-discovery parent would repeat existing direct objects; API availability or absent utility events is insufficient to create it.
- Existing-report and cache substitution can change observations, but supporting implementation alone does not prove adversarial selection or trace contrast for every information type. Existing host-knowledge reuse remains scoped to its evidence. Broader inventory, policy, file-index, time-cache and VM-cache hypotheses remain research rather than another parent.
- Event-driven acquisition needs a supported deliberate replacement of repeated inquiries and a different observation contract. Peripheral notifications have an existing home. PropertyCollector capability or a normal refresh cycle does not establish a new covert VM-subscription class.
- Sparse, distributed or filtered queries need positive attacker-control and observation evidence. Low counts, fewer rows, successful pagination or a missing event are insufficient. Target selection, platform, product, status field, format and command syntax are not independent parent taxonomies.
- Moving a lookup to a controller can have a supported observation-position effect under the established location child. Management operations outside a guest are intrinsic to managed-VM inventory and cannot themselves justify an off-guest Discovery parent or NM-BT.
- CSV/XML formatting or timezone conversion does not establish content obscuration or a parser-evasion technique. A result format needs a documented interpretation difference and stealth causality before a separate class is admitted.
- A condition that changes later execution belongs to the evidenced gate, not a duplicate fact-acquisition parent. A timer loop may consume observation time without judging an environment. Human interaction can be an ordinary execution trigger. Source code, actual comparisons and successor evidence must distinguish these cases.

## Cross-tactic and shared-library convergence

TA0011's completed fixed inventory supplies the boundary for communication carriage: querying facts and returning results do not by themselves establish a separate C2 concealment behavior. A packet label is not a disk artifact, and operating-hour configuration is not proof that its preceding time read was evasive. Transport, encoding, communication scheduling and infrastructure exposure require their own directly supported actions.

Shared references follow publication identity rather than candidate names. The fixed TrustedSec repository and BOF documentation support independently verified task modules without becoming proof of field adoption. Official clock, identity, service and management APIs describe implementation contracts rather than default telemetry. A common run coordinator can be reused across related experiments, while identity handles, SCM pagination, VM views and typed time returns require different logic. Existing measures and applications are preserved under application-only extensions; the independently reviewed NJT-0122 repair retains its measures and target identities while correcting overstated deployment/effect assertions.

## Remaining research and approval

The thirteen pre-existing deferred taxonomies were inspected through their explicit limitations and retained as evidence-bounded follow-up work, not silently relabeled complete. New candidates with plausible but unsupported axes likewise need truthful coverage states. This batch does not redevelop all prior parents, execute malware or hypervisor experiments, measure detection rates or create human reviewers.

Completing every fixed parent decision and its accepted formal outcome is distinct from closing all child hypotheses or obtaining human domain approval. The final tactic state must preserve those distinctions and remain review rather than published or unqualified complete.
